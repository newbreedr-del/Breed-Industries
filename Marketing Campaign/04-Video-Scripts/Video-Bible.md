# THE BREED INDUSTRIES — VIDEO BIBLE
## 8 Short-form Scripts (TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts / LinkedIn Video)
### Every script ends with a Breed CTA. Every script can be shot in one half-day.

**Master format rules**

- **Hook in 1.5 seconds or it dies.**
- **Vertical, 1080x1920.** Even when reposting on LinkedIn, keep the vertical version master.
- **First text overlay** within the first 0.5 second.
- **CTAs** alternate between *DM a keyword* (high reply rate) and *Visit thebreed.co.za*.
- **End frame = brand lockup**: navy bg, gold "From Idea to Empire." + URL for ~2 seconds.
- **Lower-third bar**: gold strip, white serif optional, `@breedindustries · thebreed.co.za`.
- **Fonts on screen**: Montserrat 700 / 800 only.
- **Music**: pick a TikTok-trending neutral track (instrumental). Avoid copyrighted hits.

## PRODUCTION CHECKLIST (pre-shoot)

- [ ] Backdrops: navy paper roll + warm off-white roll
- [ ] Lighting: 2 LED panels + 1 gold/amber gel for accent
- [ ] Wardrobe: dark base layer, no busy patterns, gold or cream accent piece
- [ ] B-roll: 30 minutes of studio shots, hands-on-keyboard, screens, prints, tools
- [ ] Audio: lapel mic + handheld backup; record room tone
- [ ] Slate every take, log them in a spreadsheet so the editor isn't lost
- [ ] Backup to drive **and** cloud at end of shoot day

## POST CHECKLIST (per video)

- [ ] Cut to length, drop in master end card
- [ ] Apply brand-pack lower thirds
- [ ] Burn-in captions (auto + manual cleanup)
- [ ] Audio mix: voice -3dB, music -18dB under VO
- [ ] Export: 1080x1920 master + 1080x1080 + 1920x1080 (LinkedIn / YouTube)
- [ ] Save with naming convention: `BREED_S0X_TITLE_v1.mp4`
- [ ] Drop into the social calendar in `/02-Content-Calendar/`
